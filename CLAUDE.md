## Design Context

**Brand**: Bold. Spiritual. Epic. — Cinematic missionary social deduction game for Adventist youth/young adults.

**Users**: Church youth groups, Pathfinder events, family game nights, college socials. Ages 13-30. Mobile-first (phones at group gatherings).

**Aesthetic**: Dark mode, deep navy (#0a0e1a) + gold (#d4a843) + blue (#3b82f6). Playfair Display serif headings, Inter sans body. Cinematic and atmospheric like Alto's Odyssey. NOT a generic church app or children's game.

**Design Principles**:
1. **Cinematic over decorative** — Animations serve the narrative (role reveals, mission outcomes). Nothing decorative for its own sake.
2. **Mobile-first, thumb-friendly** — Min 44px touch targets. Critical actions reachable one-handed. No hover-dependent UI.
3. **Clarity under pressure** — Status, role, and required actions immediately obvious. Players decide quickly in noisy social settings.
4. **Spiritual weight without heaviness** — Missionary theme is genuine. EG White quotes and real locations earn their place. UI stays inviting and fun.
5. **Premium feel, zero friction** — Polished enough to impress. Smooth transitions. Consistent spacing. No rough edges.
